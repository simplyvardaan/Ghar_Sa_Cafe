Dil Se Kahaani

“Ghar Jaisa Kyun?”

“Because every home has a smell.”

Ours smells like tadka hitting hot ghee,
fresh rotis puffing on the iron stove,
and chai that somehow always becomes one more cup.

We didn’t build घर सा Café to be another slick, stainless restaurant with plastic garnishes and frozen gravies. We built it because we missed home at 2 PM on a Tuesday. We missed coming into a kitchen where someone asks: “Beta, bhookh lagi hai? Do phulke aur bana doon?”

🌿

Fresh Every Morning

No pre-made frozen gravies. Our onions are chopped fresh at 6:30 AM, tomatoes slow-simmered, and greens cleaned with care.

👵

Family Recipes

Preserved on yellowed diary pages and whispered cooking secrets from nanis, daadis, and fathers who knew exactly when the heeng was fried just right.

🚫

No Shortcuts

Pure cold-pressed mustard oil, bilona cow ghee, whole roasted spices pounded in an iron mortar. No artificial food coloring or flavor enhancers. Ever.
